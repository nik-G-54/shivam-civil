Recommended website structure
1. Hero / Home

The first screen should immediately answer:

Who are we? What do we do? Where do we work?

Example:

“Building Infrastructure. Delivering Water. Connecting Communities.”

We undertake government and private infrastructure projects including water-supply systems, pipeline installation, reservoirs, overhead water tanks, roads, drainage and civil works.

Don't put too much technical information here. The visitor should understand the company within 5–10 seconds.

This is your company story.

Who We Are

We are a construction and infrastructure company undertaking government and private projects with a focus on water infrastructure, pipeline networks and civil construction.

Then a small Mission / Vision section.

Important

“We are committed to excellence and innovation.”

Every construction website says this.

Instead explain what the company actually does and what makes its execution reliable.

6. How We Work

You already mentioned this, and it's very important.

I'd make it a proper Project Execution Process.

01 — Project Planning

Understand project requirements, drawings, specifications and site conditions.

02 — Site Survey

Inspect the location and understand the ground/site requirements.

03 — Material & Resource Planning

Arrange manpower, machinery, pipes, construction materials and other resources.

04 — Execution

Carry out construction, pipeline installation, tank/reservoir work, roads etc.

05 — Quality Inspection

Check the work according to project specifications.

06 — Testing & Commissioning

Test pipelines, water systems and other infrastructure where applicable.

07 — Handover

Complete documentation and hand over the completed project.

This makes the company look like an organized contractor, rather than just a company that “does construction.”

18. Animation philosophy

Yahan main ek important correction karunga.

Images impressive hain, isliye temptation hoga:

"Chalo website mein bahut 3D animations daal dete hain."

Nahi.

Animation should be functional, not decorative.

Use:

smooth image reveal
blueprint line drawing
construction-progress transition
number counter
horizontal project movement
subtle parallax
machinery movement
pipeline flow
scroll-triggered process
hover technical details

Avoid:

random floating objects
excessive 3D
rotating buildings
huge WebGL everywhere
10 different animation styles

Existing page already uses AOS, Swiper, CountUp and parallax/Ken Burns effects.

New version ko more controlled and premium banana chahiye.
