About the company

A drilling outfit that grew into a full water-infrastructure contractor.

Sonam Sales Corporation started in 1991 as a small tubewell-drilling operation working farms and homes around Lucknow and Hardoi. Thirty-three years on, the core of the job hasn't changed — a rig, a crew, and a plot of land that needs water — but the company around it has: it now builds overhead tanks, installs rainwater harvesting structures, and carries out government contracts for road, water-supply and telecom infrastructure across Uttar Pradesh.

The company holds Government Approved status, which allows it to bid on and execute public infrastructure work alongside its private borewell and construction business. Nothing is subcontracted out — the crew that surveys a site is the crew that finishes it, using equipment the company owns and operates itself.

Clients span four sectors: domestic households needing a dependable connection, industrial units with higher-volume requirements, agricultural land where a working borewell decides whether a season's harvest comes in, and government bodies commissioning public infrastructure. Public listings on Justdial and Facebook put the company at a 4.6-out-of-5 rating and a 92% recommendation score — built up one borehole and one government contract at a time.
